# Face Reality — Senna Lore Compendium

*Updated Working Lore — Canon, Provisional Concepts, and Open Development*

This document consolidates the established Senna material for Face Reality. It preserves the distinction between locked canon, provisional/running concepts, and open areas. Ideas identified only as visual inspiration are not treated as borrowed lore.

# 1. Canon Status Guide

- LOCKED CANON — Established material to preserve unless deliberately revised.

- PROVISIONAL / RUNNING CONCEPT — Active direction that may still change.

- OPEN — Intentionally undecided; do not silently fill in.

- VISUAL INSPIRATION — Aesthetic reference only; no outside lore is imported.

# 2. Senna — Core Identity

Status: LOCKED CANON

Senna is a planet and civilization in Face Reality whose culture is deeply shaped by movement, engineering, racing, status earned through mastery, and the pursuit of pushing beyond one's present limits.

The name Senna is an out-of-universe tribute to Ayrton Senna. In-universe, its cultural meaning is associated with the idea of moving beyond, pursuing the horizon, and the phrase “Keep Pushing.”

The underlying philosophy is not that the fastest machine automatically wins. Mastery belongs to the pilot: control, adaptability, precision, commitment, resilience, risk awareness, and the ability to extract more from a machine than its specifications suggest.

## The Senna Gesture

Status: LOCKED CANON, deeper origin OPEN

A universal Sennan gesture moves a hand from near the chest outward while opening it. Depending on context it can mean good luck, safe travels, stay strong, keep going, or general goodwill. Its deeper historical symbolism remains intentionally unexplained for now.

# 3. Momentum Lines

Status: LOCKED CORE CONCEPT; detailed physics OPEN

Momentum Lines are natural, dangerous, high-speed atmospheric currents that can carry craft at extreme speed while reducing the propulsion normally required to maintain that velocity. They are fundamental to Sennan racing, travel, engineering, and cultural identity.

- The fastest routes through Momentum Lines are also among the most dangerous.

- Different ship builds can exploit different Lines and routes rather than one build being universally best.

- Piloting skill matters: entry angle, speed, control, stabilization, and judgment should determine how effectively a racer uses a Line.

- Experienced Sennans can read environmental signs that reveal the presence or direction of a Line.

Still open: whether Lines migrate, disappear, change seasonally, how they are detected technologically, exact failure states, and the full consequences of entering one incorrectly.

# 4. The Worldline

Status: LOCKED CORE CONCEPT; some route details OPEN

The Worldline is Senna's enormous prestige race and one of the civilization's defining aspirations. It crosses a broad range of terrain and tests far more than raw top speed.

- Potential route environments include cities, mountains, canyons, storms, caves, open atmosphere, and other extreme terrain.

- A segment that briefly exits and re-enters the atmosphere remains optional rather than locked.

- The course should reward different builds, routes, piloting styles, and environmental understanding.

The Worldline represents Sennan philosophy at its largest scale: mastery is proven through adaptation, control, endurance, and the willingness to keep pushing.

# 5. Environment and Planetary Visual Identity

Status: LOCKED CANON

Senna is not a single-biome planet. Its environment is defined by dramatic transitions and terrain that naturally feels like a racecourse: sharp mountain chains, vast canyon systems, enormous open plains, rocky regions with arches and tunnels, storm belts, caves, cliffs, and high-altitude formations.

Water is relatively uncommon inland without making Senna a pure desert world. Large seas or oceans may exist, while much of the racing civilization developed across continents dominated by mountains, valleys, plains, cliffs, and atmospheric formations.

## Momentum-Shaped Landscape

- Mountains and stone may be polished or carved into directional curves by centuries of atmospheric forces.

- Vegetation may permanently lean with prevailing currents.

- Natural stone arches and channels can trace ancient or active Momentum routes.

- Clouds can form long ribbons that visually reveal atmospheric movement.

## Cities and Terrain

Sennan civilization should look grown with the environment rather than placed on top of it. Buildings can wrap cliffs, transportation corridors can intersect racing routes, viewing platforms can overlook natural speedways, and settlements may exist because important Momentum Lines intersect nearby.

Guiding visual rule: Senna should look fast even when nothing is moving.

## Visual Inspiration

The world of Zeffo from Jedi: Fallen Order is a cosmetic and environmental inspiration only. The useful visual language includes weathered stone, monumental ancient forms, cliffs, ruins integrated into terrain, and a sense of an older civilization beneath the present one. No Zeffo lore, history, species, or story concepts carry over.

# 6. Ancient Layer of Senna

Status: Ancient statues are an ESTABLISHED NOTE; origin and meaning OPEN

Ancient statues are scattered across the planet. They should contribute to the feeling that modern Senna exists on top of a much older and only partly understood history.

Their builders, age, purpose, subjects, connection to early Sennan culture, and relationship—if any—to Momentum Lines remain open.

# 7. Racing Culture and Historical Foundation

Status: PROVISIONAL CANON unless otherwise marked

Momentum Lines created regional racing traditions long before Senna was politically unified. Racing became a shared language between otherwise separate regions.

Alaric Sovereign did not literally invent racing. He recognized its power as a common Sennan language, helped formalize shared rules and standards of mastery, and used those traditions as part of the process that unified Senna. Later generations may mythologize him as the father of modern Sennan racing.

A core social idea is that position should follow demonstrated mastery and service rather than simple possession of wealth or machinery. Competition does not require hatred; a worthy opponent can be respected, and recognizing another person's fair victory is compatible with personal honor.

# 8. Alaric Sovereign

Status: LOCKED CHARACTER DIRECTION; some historical details remain PROVISIONAL/OPEN

Alaric Sovereign is Senna's first great ruler and a foundational figure in the planet's political and racing history. House Sovereign is now nearly absent—a cultural ghost—but traditions associated with Alaric continue to shape Senna.

## Locked Personality

Alaric carries a restrained Lando Calrissian-like presence: smooth, charismatic, stylish, confident, socially sharp, and capable of commanding a room without behaving overly stern or regal. The influence is tonal rather than a copy of the character.

His defining trait is honor above all else. Charm is how he carries himself; honor is what drives him. He respects worthy opponents, keeps his word even when it costs him, accepts a fair defeat, and expects integrity from others.

A philosophy associated with him is that recognizing another person's victory does not diminish your own honor; refusing to recognize a fair victory does.

## Historical / Environmental Storytelling

- A hidden, questless environmental discovery can lead to Alaric's tomb.

- An elder storyteller flashback can reveal the origins of Senna's hierarchy and show Alaric's racer.

- The exact contents, location, and significance of the tomb remain open unless separately established.

# 10. Major-City Social Hierarchy

Status: PROVISIONAL / RUNNING CITY CONCEPT

A major Sennan city should contain an inner-city hierarchy that is largely unspoken. The player feels the social transition while moving inward rather than receiving an exposition dump.

- Outer districts are working areas: mechanics, racers, markets, transit, workshops, families, and ordinary daily life.

- Toward the center, the city becomes cleaner, older, quieter, more deliberate, and more closely associated with powerful Houses, respected families, elite racers, officials, and the Crown.

- Status is not simply 'rich versus poor.' Old nobility, House influence, wealth, reputation, service, and earned mastery overlap.

- The closer one gets to the heart of the city, the less status needs to be advertised because the people there already know who matters.

## Matriarchal Undertone

The inner city may carry an unspoken matriarchal social influence without declaring Senna a formal matriarchy. The player can gradually notice that many influential House elders, family heads, patrons, and ceremonial authorities are women. The Crown can remain a separate institution from that social tradition.

## Visual Transition

- Less advertising and clutter toward the center.

- More monumental and older architecture.

- Private gardens or courtyards.

- House symbols integrated subtly into buildings.

- Ceremonial clothing becomes more common.

- Behavior becomes more formal without requiring explicit signage or barriers.

# 11. Clothing and Personal Style

Status: RUNNING CONCEPT — NOT LOCKED

Sennan clothing should reinforce movement, mastery, House identity, and tradition without becoming generic glowing sci-fi fashion. Technology is advanced but visually restrained.

## Daily Clothing

- Fitted, practical silhouettes designed around movement.

- Short jackets or split-tail coats, layered shirts, fitted trousers, boots for uneven terrain, gloves or forearm pieces.

- Scarves, wraps, or fabric elements that visibly react to Senna's wind.

- Subtle smart fabrics, protective materials, fastening systems, and communications technology.

- House identity appears through stitching, clasps, shoulder pieces, fabric cuts, jewelry, colors, and materials rather than obvious uniforms.

## Formal and Ceremonial Clothing

- Longer coats, robes, or mantles split to preserve movement.

- Structured shoulders, layered fabric over fitted clothing, metal clasps, and House insignia.

- Garments should become more visually impressive when the wearer moves.

- Ceremonial fabric may be deliberately designed to catch wind or react dramatically near Momentum Lines.

## Racing Ceremonies

- Competitors may wear formalized versions of racing attire rather than Earth-style suits and dresses.

- Possible elements include tailored racing coats, ceremonial gloves, achievement sashes or mantles, collars, clasps, and jewelry tied to major victories.

## Crown Attire

Rulers should look controlled rather than decadent: precise long coats or mantles, restrained insignia, and selected ancient ceremonial elements that may predate the current House.

Guiding clothing rule: Sennan clothing should look elegant standing still, but look its best in motion.

# 12. Mechanics

Status: PROVISIONAL

Three mechanic archetypes represent different philosophies of building a racer. They should remain useful rather than forming a simple bad-to-good progression.

## Budget / Scrapper Mechanic

Searches for cheap, discarded, salvaged, or overlooked components and takes pride in proving that expensive equipment does not automatically make a great machine. This mechanic can discover unconventional combinations and embodies the idea that skill and ingenuity matter more than price.

## Adrenaline Mechanic

Adventurous, experimental, and performance-obsessed. Not necessarily irresponsible, but eager to push equipment beyond conventional expectations. Their work can favor unusual acceleration, handling, Momentum performance, or extreme builds with meaningful tradeoffs.

## High-End Mechanic

A precision specialist trusted with genuinely rare, legendary, or technically demanding components. Their workspace and methods should feel immaculate and specialized. Some exceptional parts may require this level of equipment, reputation, or expertise to install correctly.

# 13. The Player's Ship and Racing Progression

Status: WORKING MODEL / PROVISIONAL

The player's racer is ultimately the player's own ship. Senna does not permanently replace it with a uniquely Sennan craft.

At the beginning of the Senna racing progression, the player's ship is not race-ready. The player gains a basic set of starter racing parts that make the ship sufficiently compatible with beginner races and Momentum Lines. As the player advances through Sennan races, exploration, rewards, and mechanic relationships, they acquire increasingly specialized racer parts.

- Parts modify the existing personal ship rather than replacing it.

- Possible upgrade areas include propulsion, stabilization, maneuvering, Momentum Line control, cooling, boost capacity, structural systems, and specialized components.

- Progression should not be only Starter Part → Better Part → Best Part.

- Some parts should change how the ship races, enabling distinct builds centered on acceleration, control, durability, Line efficiency, top speed, or other styles.

- The three mechanic archetypes can support different build philosophies.

The intended arc is that the player arrives with their own ship and gradually turns that same ship into a capable Sennan racer through choices, earned parts, and mastery.

A genuine Sennan starter racer may still be used as an introductory learning experience, but the long-term progression centers on upgrading the player's own ship.

# 14. Gameplay Philosophy on Senna

Status: ESTABLISHED DIRECTION

- Racing should teach environmental movement skills that remain useful during broader exploration.

- The best equipment should not automatically beat the best pilot.

- Course knowledge, Momentum Line reading, control, risk management, and build choice should matter.

- Different ship builds should solve the same course in different ways.

- Mechanics, racing rewards, exploration, and ship customization should form one connected progression loop.

# 21. Continuity Notes

- Senna's portal/dimension-switching gameplay idea is not part of the active Senna design. The broader Face Reality history may contain portal events elsewhere, but Senna should not be built around a dimension-switching mechanic.

- The Zeffo reference is strictly visual inspiration and carries no lore.

- The Senna gesture's deeper origin remains unexplained.

- The Worldline space segment remains optional.

- Both former uses of the name “The Veil” elsewhere in Face Reality are retired and should remain placeholder names until deliberately renamed; they are separate factions.

- New Senna material should be checked against locked canon before being treated as final.
