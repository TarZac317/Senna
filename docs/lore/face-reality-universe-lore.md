# Face Reality — Game Universe Lore

*Cyberpunk • Post-Apocalyptic Science Fiction • Dark Fantasy*

WORKING DRAFT — story details are preserved as currently established; proposed names are labeled.

# The Premise and Atmosphere

An original game universe blending an oppressive, flourishing cyberpunk society with a ruined, fog-heavy wasteland and a parallel world of magic. The desired atmosphere is industrial, gothic, bleak, and tense, with advanced machinery, monstrous threats, supernatural forces, and societies shaped by fear and inherited catastrophe. Gameplay is intended to support both first-person and third-person perspectives.

# ACT 1 — ALEX AND THE FALL OF THE OLD WORLD

## The Wealthy

A ruling faction, provisionally called the Wealthy, controls an entire continent. Their power is based not on money but on DNA: systems, infrastructure, and technological advancements are genetically locked, allowing them to maintain control. They are not immortal, but can live for thousands of years; they are largely free of disease, and failing organs can be replaced with cloned organs. The civilization appears prosperous and technologically advanced, while its rulers become increasingly corrupt and bored by their own dominance.

The continent is enclosed by massive walls and guarded formations because of monsters beyond them. The creatures are extraordinarily resilient: their bones are made from a steel-composite-like material, so a headshot causes no damage. Across hundreds of years, the Wealthy expand aggressively, using soldiers to fight the monsters and extend the walls farther into the territory beyond.

## The Firstborn and the Robotic Soldiers

Every family must surrender its firstborn when that child reaches their twenties. The public is told that this service is necessary to protect the continent from the monsters. Families do not know the real purpose or fate of the people taken. The outcome is random: some are frozen or held in suspended sleep for five years and then returned to their families with no recollection of the interval because they were asleep. Others are killed, their brains harvested to operate robotic soldiers, and their bodies discarded. The population does not know that conscripts are being turned into machinery.

The robotic soldiers retain little of their former selves. Their core directives are to follow their leaders, defend their leaders, and carry out orders without question.

## The Last Werewolf and Alex's Awakening

After centuries of expansion, one monster species—the werewolves—is close to extinction. The Wealthy capture the last known werewolf and attempt to breed it for their own amusement and prestige. The attempt goes wrong and the creature escapes into the population, killing roughly one or two thousand people. The disaster becomes too large to cover up, so the Wealthy send their robotic soldiers against the populace.

One soldier, called Alex for now, kills his own parents while carrying out the orders. When he sees his little sister, his programming goes haywire and he regains control of his consciousness. Horrified by what he has done, he understands what is happening. He is crushed during the events, but manages to save part of the population. Alex is the player character throughout Act 1. His full name is not final; “Alex Veyr” is an assistant-proposed working name.

## The Scientist, the Uprising, and the Hidden Truth

Among those Alex saves is one of the civilization's leading scientists and engineers, who has actively worked on the system. The scientist sees how power has corrupted the Wealthy and recognizes that Alex's return to consciousness is extraordinarily rare—roughly one in a billion. The scientist equips Alex with a neural interface that allows him to control other robotic soldiers. With their help, Alex and the survivors overthrow the Wealthy.

After the overthrow, Alex discovers a darker secret: the harvested bodies of conscripts were not simply disposed of. They were processed into sustenance, and human remains were used to cultivate soil as the civilization expanded. In an earlier age, nuclear weapons had been used in an attempt to eradicate the monsters, but failed to eliminate them and left the land beyond the walls barren, destroying ordinary food sources. The wasteland was being cultivated bit by bit using human remains.

Alex decides that the world has become uninhabitable and orders the Wealthy killed. Some escape off-world in spacecraft that are DNA-locked. Others retreat into underground bunkers prepared for catastrophic situations, stocked with weapons and systems able to control weapons across the continent. The once-prosperous city becomes another wasteland.

## The Portal and the Parallel World

Years pass. Alex gathers the cleverest people among the survivors and asks them to create a portal to an alternative universe. After roughly a decade or two, they succeed. The parallel world is filled with magic, monsters, demons, and humans. Its kingdoms are in a tense, Cold War-like standoff rather than open war, with everyone prepared for conflict. Its technology is much less advanced; swords and bows remain common.

Alex and a few other robots travel through the portal. He meets a king and explains the suffering and hardships of his people. The king offers to let the population settle in his kingdom in return for access to advanced machinery and help defending the kingdom. Alex refuses to exchange one master for another. They compromise: the king may have a select group of top scientists teach his people to create advanced weaponry, and the refugees may settle there.

## Protocol Scorched Earth and the Catastrophe

As Alex prepares to return and bring the population through the portal, a scientist recommends a backup plan in case Alex is deactivated. They create PROTOCOL SCORCHED EARTH: upon Alex's deactivation, the robots are to fight fiercely to defend his sister and the populace. The written protocol accidentally omits the word “AND,” leaving a critical ambiguity.

Alex returns with scientists and a group of robots and tells the population to prepare to move—millions of people are involved. The king invites the delegation to sign a treaty as a formality. A demon spy, who has been hiding in the crowd since learning of the parallel world's existence, bombs the castle with hellfire. Alex and the delegation are killed, and the signal is cut off. On the original-world side, the robots treat the protocol as activated and begin killing everyone except themselves and Alex's sister.

For months, knights and wizards fight through the portal to seize the advanced technology. They eventually capture the facility but cannot understand the unfamiliar machinery and language. Their attempts to operate the engine overheat it, causing an explosion that destroys the facility and portal. They manage to move aside before the blast. The two worlds are cut off from one another.

# ACT 2 — THIRTY YEARS LATER

The playable era begins thirty years after the catastrophe. The portal is gone and Alex is dead. The robots continue locating and destroying other living beings. The factions are in constant war with one another while also trying to survive the robot threat. Players can choose a starting character from one of three factions.

## Playable Factions and the Robot Threat

THE REMNANTS — Descendants of the Wealthy. They retain their precious DNA and fragments of the old privileged legacy, but generations of inbreeding have corrupted their bloodlines.

THE MAGICAL FACTION — Descendants of the knights and wizards who crossed into the technological world. They are warriors who can wield weapons. The final faction name is undecided; “The Gloam Covenant” is a proposed working name.

THE VEIL — Descendants of the original populace. They are barely surviving and rely on stealth to stay alive.

THE ROBOTIC SOLDIERS — They continue to locate and destroy any other living being. “The Null” is a proposed working name for the robot army; its current interpretation of the original directives remains to be defined.

# Open Mysteries and Working Names

- Alex's sister's fate remains a mystery.

- The exact technical reason Protocol Scorched Earth causes the robots to turn on the populace remains to be defined.

- The Wealthy, Alex's full name, the magical faction, and the robot army may receive different final names.

- The future plans of the Wealthy who escaped off-world or retreated underground remain unknown.

- The origins and full nature of the monsters remain to be developed.
